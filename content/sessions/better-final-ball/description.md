Your final ball isn't as good as the rest of your game. There's a reason for that — and it's got nothing to do with talent.

Think about how many reps you get of the things you're best at. The passes in the middle of the park. The dribbling. Taking players on. Hundreds a week, every week, for years.

Now think about how many reps you get of the moment you've broken free, the pressure's on, and the whole thing is on your foot.

Nowhere near as many.

That's it. That's the whole reason. Your brain conditions what it repeats — and you simply haven't had the exposure on this one.

This session gives you that exposure.

## What this session conditions

- Anticipating the opportunity before it opens up — seeing it early, moving with purpose
- Clarity in the moment others rush — the picture stays clear, the options stay visible
- Instant, high-quality decision making when the final ball is on
- Full, committed execution — your pass, your cross, your shot, at your very best level
- Continued involvement right through to the conclusion of the play

## How it works

Guided visualisation and hypnotherapy get you zoned in, immersed and fully in the game. Then you go to work — repetition after repetition of being in exactly the moments where your final ball decides the game.

You're guided through it, then handed 45-second intervals to run your own passages of play, over and over, completely immersed and without my voice. Then I come back in.

Every rehearsal builds the pathway. That's how the brain develops a skill — and it's why this works.

## Before you start

Have a think about where your big opportunities usually come from in a game. Those are the passages you'll be conditioning.

Talk to your coaches about the specifics — who you should be looking for, what your movement should be. If you can't, watch what the best players in the world do in those situations. YouTube, Wyscout if you have it.

Bring those specifics in. The more real they are, the more powerfully this conditions.

Use it daily. Use it before training. Use it whenever you're ready to put the work in.

The work others don't do — so you reach the levels others can't.
