## Who this is for

The central midfielder who wants to run the game. Not just be in it — run it.

The tempo, the angles, the ball going where you decide it goes.

## What's actually happening

The midfielders who dominate aren't doing more. They're seeing more, earlier — and their attention is out in the game.

- They know the picture before the ball arrives. The players who scan more before receiving complete more passes. The decision is made before the first touch, so the ball moves quicker than the press can close it.
- Their attention points outwards. Every best game you've ever played came with your attention out in the game — the ball, the space, your next action. Turn it inward, onto how you're playing, and your football stops being instinctive.
- They read what's about to happen. Across sport, expert players are better at picking up the cues that tell them what comes next. That's anticipation — and in the middle of the park, a split second earlier is everything.

## What this session conditions

- Getting your picture early — shoulders checked, options known, before the ball arrives
- Receiving on the half turn and playing forward, at your tempo
- Setting the rhythm of the game — when to speed it up, when to take the sting out
- Showing for the ball, always available, always an angle
- Winning your duels and your second balls, and doing something with them
- Your attention out in the game for the full ninety minutes, right through to the end of every passage

## How it works

Guided visualisation and hypnotherapy get you immersed and fully in the game. Then you go to work.

Imagining yourself playing engages much of the same motor planning circuitry in your brain as playing does — so the football itself is being trained, repetition after repetition, without a ball or a pitch.

You're guided through it, then handed 45-second intervals to run your own passages of play, over and over, without my voice. Then I come back in.

## How to prepare

Have this ready before you start:

- The passages of play where you want to take control — building from the back, breaking the press, the transition
- Your job in your team's shape. Talk to your coaches about the specifics
- What it looks like when you're dominating. Watch the best midfielders in the world — how often they look around, where they receive, how quickly they play

Use it daily. Use it before training. Use it before a game.
