## Who this is for

Every player. Whether you've got the enjoyment and want more of it, or the game has turned into a chore and you want it back.

Enjoying your football isn't the reward for playing well. It's one of the things that makes you play well.

## What's actually happening

Five things, and none of them is about feeling better for its own sake:

- You learn faster. When something goes well and it feels good, your brain releases dopamine — and dopamine arriving in your motor cortex is what allows a movement to be locked in as a skill. Reward the same practice and it gets learned better and held onto longer. Two players do the same session; the one enjoying it comes away with more of it.
- You see more of the game. Your attention has a scope — how wide or narrow it is — and it isn't fixed. Feeling under threat narrows it. Enjoying yourself widens it. Same eyes, same pitch, more of the picture. That's what your decisions come out of.
- Your energy is higher. This has been measured as its own thing, separately from how hard you've physically worked. Players put in enormous physical effort and still come off feeling alive — when the football is something they've chosen and they're enjoying it. Do the same running without it and it drains you.
- Your drive is higher, and it's a different thing again. You can have energy and not be driven to use it. When you're doing something because you want to, you go harder at it and you stay at it longer. Found consistently.
- It's one of the characteristics of the zone. The flow state — the blue performance state — enjoyment is one of the things that's there when you're in it. It's part of what that state is.

## What this session conditions

- The association between your football and the enjoyment of playing it
- That feeling running through your game as you play it, not sitting alongside it
- Your football as it is now — your position, your job in the team, the structure you play in — with the enjoyment right through it
- Holding it deep into a game, when there's more riding on it and more intensity around you
- Full involvement through the whole passage of play, on and off the ball, to its conclusion

## How it works

Guided visualisation and hypnotherapy get you immersed and fully in the game. Then you go to work.

You start where the love of it was strongest — the park, the academy, whenever it was for you. You relive those moments, over and over.

Then you bring that same feeling forward into your football now. Your next session, your next game, played exactly as you play it — with the enjoyment running through all of it.

Every rehearsal builds the association. That's how the brain conditions anything, and it's why this works.

You're guided through it, then handed 45-second intervals to run your own passages of play, over and over, without my voice. Then I come back in.

## How to prepare

Have this ready before you start:

- The football you fell in love with. Not a result and not a highlight — the feeling of being out there playing it.
- Where that was strongest. Playing as a kid, the academy, youth football, wherever it was for you.
- The moments. Where you were on the pitch, what was happening, what you were doing. Training or games, either works.
- Choose them before you begin, not once we've started.

Use it daily. Use it before training. Use it before a game.
