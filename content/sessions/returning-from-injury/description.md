You've been cleared. Your body is ready.

But its unlikely you're going to be back to your usual self straight away. Maybe something still doesn't feel quite right. There's a hesitation that wasn't there before. A fraction of a second where you hold back. A part of you still bracing for something your medical team has already told you is healed.

That is not weakness. That is your nervous system running the same protective response it conditioned during your injury.

This session is built for exactly this moment — reconditioning the brain and nervous system to match where your body already is.

Condition full, fluid, natural movement through complete range — including movements that involve the previously injured area

Recondition the nervous system's protective threat response — the internal pull that changes your timing, your body shape, and your commitment to actions

Restore the rhythm of the game — the reading, the reacting, the way your movement flows when you're fully in it

Rehearse your game at full expression inside real match intensity — the pace, the contact, the demands of it

Use from the day you are medically cleared — the sooner the brain starts conditioning for return, the faster that return feels like you

Before each session, bring one specific area of your game to mind — one movement, one pattern, one aspect of how you play at your best. Stay with the same focus or vary it each time

The intensive version is your sharp, focused conditioning tool — perfect before training sessions as well as when you're ready to put the hard work in for a focused session.

The relaxation version gives you added conditioning when you are recovering, relaxing and you simply want some down time, but want to make progress at the same time.

The more consistently you use these session, the more you'll be ready when match day comes.
