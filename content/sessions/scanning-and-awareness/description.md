## Who this is for

Every player who receives the ball with players around them. Midfielders most of all — but full backs, centre backs and forwards too.

If the ball keeps arriving and you're finding out what's around you after it's at your feet, this is the session.

## What's actually happening

The best decisions in football are made before the ball arrives.

- A player gets their picture by turning their head away from the ball to look around them. Researchers call it scanning. In Premier League match data, the players who scan more before receiving complete more passes.
- The picture you take before the ball arrives is the picture you play from. Receive without it and you're facing the wrong way, with less of the game in front of you than you should have. The pass that follows is worse — and it's got nothing to do with how good you are.
- Under pressure, scanning drops. When an opponent is tight to you, you look around less — exactly when you need it most.
- Scanning isn't a trick you remember to do. At the level it needs to happen, it has to be automatic — part of how you play, not something you think about.

## What this session conditions

- Checking your shoulders before the ball arrives, automatically, every time it could be coming to you
- Knowing where the space is, where your team mates are and where the pressure is coming from — before your first touch
- Receiving on the half turn, already facing where the game is going
- Playing forward quicker, because the decision was made before the ball got to you
- Keeping it going under pressure, with a player tight to you, deep into the game
- Staying in the passage of play right through to its conclusion

## How it works

Guided visualisation and hypnotherapy get you immersed and fully in the game. Then you go to work.

Imagining yourself playing engages much of the same motor planning circuitry in your brain as playing does. So the scanning, the picture and the decision are being trained — real repetitions, without a ball or a pitch.

You're guided through it, then handed 45-second intervals to run your own passages of play, over and over, without my voice. Then I come back in.

## How to prepare

Have this ready before you start:

- Where you receive the ball most in your games — the areas of the pitch and the situations
- Where the pressure usually comes from when you're there
- What the picture looks like when you get it right — the pass you can play when you've seen it early

Watch the best players in your position. Watch their heads before the ball arrives, not the ball. YouTube, Wyscout if you have it.

Use it daily. Use it before training. Use it before a game.
