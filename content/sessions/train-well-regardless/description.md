There will be sessions where you don't feel like it. Where the motivation isn't there, you're out of favour, or you're simply not feeling it that day.

Most players think that's a motivation problem. It isn't. It's a reference point problem.

When your effort is driven by external conditions — selection, the manager, comparison with teammates — your training quality is hostage to those conditions. This session shifts that reference point from external to internal, and conditions it until it becomes your default.

## What this session conditions

- Arriving at every session with your own internal standard already set — your best today, in whatever the session contains, applied to everything
- Full immersion in each drill — completely in it, competing and working at the level you've set
- The reset — the brief internal check in the natural breaks between drills that keeps the standard holding regardless of what's happening around you

## What that produces

- Training quality that doesn't depend on external conditions — consistent, regardless of circumstance
- Development that compounds session after session — your game growing precisely because your standard doesn't pause when things are difficult
- The form, the recognition, the selection — these follow from a player who kept developing when others didn't

## When to use it

- Before training — particularly when motivation is low, things aren't going your way, or you need to reconnect with your own standard
- During recovery or downtime — to condition the approach ahead of the next session
- Before sleep — the relaxation version works here
- Two versions: the intensive for focused conditioning before training, the relaxation version for recovery, downtime, or before sleep.

The more consistently you use this, the more deeply the internal standard becomes conditioned — until arriving at training ready to go, regardless of circumstance, is simply what you do.
