# Fearless Footballer — Beta Tester Onboarding & Distribution Guide

**Version:** 1.0.0 (Beta Release)  
**Target Platforms:** iOS (Apple TestFlight) & Android (Google Play Internal Testing)  
**Target Audience:** Athletes (ages 10–17) and Caregivers / Parents

---

## 1. Overview

Welcome to the **Fearless Footballer** Beta Testing Program! Fearless Footballer is a mental composure, rehearsal, and focus training app designed specifically for young soccer athletes.

This guide details how to install the beta app on your iOS or Android device, complete guardian authorization, test interactive sessions, and provide feedback to the engineering team.

---

## 2. Installation Instructions

### 🍎 iOS (Apple TestFlight)
1. Install **TestFlight** from the Apple App Store on your iPhone.
2. Open your email invite or click the official TestFlight public link: `https://testflight.apple.com/join/fearlessfootballer`.
3. Tap **Accept** and then tap **Install**.
4. Open **Fearless Footballer** from your home screen.

### 🤖 Android (Google Play Internal Testing)
1. Join the Google Group or click the Google Play Internal Testing opt-in link sent via email.
2. Tap **Become a Tester**.
3. Click **Download it on Google Play** to install the app via the Play Store.
4. Open **Fearless Footballer** from your app drawer.

---

## 3. Account Setup & Parental Consent (COPPA / GDPR-K)

### For Athletes (Under 18)
1. Select **Athlete Role** on the welcome screen.
2. Enter your birth date during onboarding.
3. If under 13, you will receive a 6-character claim code (e.g. `FF-8921`) to give to your parent or guardian.
4. Once your caregiver claims and approves your account, your interactive pre-game session packages will unlock!

### For Caregivers / Parents
1. Select **Caregiver Role** on the welcome screen.
2. Enter the 6-character claim code provided by your athlete.
3. Review COPPA privacy disclosures and confirm guardian consent.
4. View your athlete's composure score trends, session completion streaks, and weekly mindset milestones from your Caregiver Dashboard.

---

## 4. Key Features to Test

- **Interactive Pre-Match Session**: Play through *"Nerves = Performance"* with audio guidance, interval prompts, phase transitions, and lockscreen controls.
- **Mindset Reflection**: Reflect on your feeling after the session (*Clearer*, *Steadier*, *More Ready*). Private reflection notes remain 100% private on your device.
- **Offline Mode**: Turn on Airplane Mode, complete a session, turn network back on, and verify that your completion automatically syncs to your streak!
- **In-App Feedback**: Use the in-app feedback dialog to send bug reports or feature ideas directly to the dev team.

---

## 5. Reporting Feedback & Issues

- **In-App Submission**: Tap the feedback icon or navigate to Settings > Submit Beta Feedback.
- **TestFlight Feedback**: Take a screenshot anytime inside the app and select **Share Beta Feedback** to attach a screenshot with native system logs.
- **Direct Support Email**: `beta@fearlessfootballer.com`.
