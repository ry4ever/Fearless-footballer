# Accessibility verification checklist

- Run every journey with VoiceOver and TalkBack.
- Verify meaningful labels and focus order for buttons, inputs, choice cards, progress, errors, and navigation.
- Verify 200% font scaling does not clip or hide required content.
- Verify minimum touch targets of 44×44 pt on iOS and 48×48 dp on Android.
- Verify normal text contrast is at least 4.5:1 under WCAG AA.
- Record device, OS, app build, screen, and pass/fail evidence.
