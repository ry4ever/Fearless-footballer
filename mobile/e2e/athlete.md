# Athlete E2E acceptance scenario

- Register an athlete account with valid profile information.
- Complete the age gate and reach the athlete home screen.
- Start the available rehearsal and verify playback controls, phases, and prompts.
- Play at least 80% of the session, complete the private reflection, and save.
- Verify the completion summary updates the athlete progress and streak.
- Sign out and verify the athlete route guard returns to the welcome screen.
